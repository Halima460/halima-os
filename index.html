Hi, I'm Halima.

I'm someone who is fascinated by people, systems, and the little things that shape our everyday experiences.

I love asking questions like:

  • Why do some services work beautifully while others don't?
  • How can we make learning more accessible for children?
  • What would happen if we designed things with people in mind from the very beginning?

Professionally, I've found myself drawn to education, customer experience, service design, and projects that improve the way people interact with the world around them.

Personally, I'm an ideas person. I can spend hours planning future projects, researching random topics, organising information into colour-coded systems, or dreaming about ways to make meaningful change.

At my core, I care deeply about helping people feel seen, supported, and understood.

I'm still figuring life out, learning, growing, changing my mind occasionally, but I'm excited about who I'm becoming.
